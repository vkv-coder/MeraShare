Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What We Collect

  • Your name and email (used to sign in and identify you to your group members)
  • Groups you create or join, and their members' display names
  • Expenses you log — description, amount, category, who paid, who it was split with, and any reference/bill number you add
  • Settlement records (who paid whom, how much)
  • Photos you upload to a trip's gallery
  • If you try the demo: your name and phone number (used only to let us know someone's exploring the demo)

We do not collect your location, contacts, or any device data beyond what's listed above.

Where Your Data Lives

All data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in the Mumbai (India) region. It is not transferred, stored, or processed outside India.

Who Can Access Your Data

Only members of a group can see that group's expenses, balances, members, and photos. Your groups are never visible to other MeraShare users outside them. Your password is stored securely (hashed) — we cannot read it.

Biometric App Lock

If you turn on fingerprint/Face ID unlock, the biometric credential is created and stored only on your own device (via your browser's platform authenticator) — it is never sent to us or stored on our servers. It's purely a local convenience to skip re-typing your password; your real account access is still controlled by your email and password underneath.

Data Deletion

You can remove a trip from your own view anytime (Activity tab → Manage trips) without affecting other members. To request complete deletion of your account and all associated data, write to info.anyapps@gmail.com — we'll delete it within 7 working days.
