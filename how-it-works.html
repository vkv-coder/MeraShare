Welcome to MeraShare

MeraShare keeps shared expenses fair and easy to track — for trips, flats, families or any group that splits costs together. Every expense is split independently: pick who was actually involved in *that* expense, and how (equally, by exact amount, or by percentage) — someone who joined a day late or skipped a meal is just unchecked for that one expense, nothing else.

Try this exact flow

  1. Open Goa Trip 2026 from your groups list — it's just you (Demo User), Rahul and Simran, no expenses yet.
  2. Tap + Add Expense. Log something you (Demo User) paid for, split Equal Share, but try unchecking one member — e.g. Simran joined a day later and wasn't there for this one.
  3. Add a second expense, paid by Rahul this time, and split it Different Amount or Different % instead — again, uncheck anyone who isn't sharing that particular cost.
  4. Check Balances — see exactly who owes who, based only on what you just added.
  5. Tap Settle Up and record Rahul or Simran paying their share to whoever they owe. Watch the balance update immediately.
