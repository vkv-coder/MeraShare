Welcome to MeraShare

MeraShare keeps shared expenses fair and easy to track — for trips, flats, families or any group that splits costs together.

Things to try

  • Open Goa Trip 2026 from your groups list — it already has real sample expenses in it.
  • Check the Balances section on the group page to see who owes who, at a glance.
  • Tap Add Expense and try all three split types: Equal (divide evenly), Exact (type each person's share), and Percentage (split by %).
  • Tap Settle Up to record a payment someone made to clear their balance.
  • Create your own group from the dashboard and share its invite code with friends.
