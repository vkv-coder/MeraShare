Welcome to MeraShare

MeraShare keeps shared expenses fair and easy to track — for trips, flats, families or any group that splits costs together.

Try this exact flow

  1. Open Goa Trip 2026 from your groups list. Scroll to Balances — Demo User (you) is already owed money from the sample expenses Rahul and Simran logged.
  2. Scroll to Members and use "Add member by email" to add yourself as a new person — type any name and a made-up email, e.g. "Priya" / priya@test.com. Now there are 4 people in the group.
  3. Tap + Add Expense. Log something the new person ("Priya") paid for — pick them as "Paid by" — and split it Equally across everyone.
  4. Add a second expense, but this time split it Exact or Percentage instead, to see how those work.
  5. Check Balances again — "Priya" now shows up owing or being owed money, alongside Demo User, Rahul and Simran.
  6. Tap Settle Up and record "Priya" paying her share to whoever she owes (e.g. Demo User). Watch the balance update immediately.
  7. When you're ready for a real group (not shared with other visitors), create a free account from the Home page.
